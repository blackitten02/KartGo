🎫 What is a Queue Token?

A Queue Token is a numbered virtual ticket designed for customers who don't own a smartphone or simply prefer the traditional cashier experience. Here's how it works:

  • You receive a token number (e.g. #105) instantly — no standing in a physical line
  • The live board shows whose number is currently being served — keep browsing the store while you wait
  • When your number appears on the board, walk to the dedicated cashier counter
  • The cashier scans all your items and processes payment normally
  • This system helps the store manage peak-hour capacity (weekends, Diwali, etc.) without crowding
  • Have a smartphone? Use self-scan instead and skip this queue entirely — checkout in <2 min!
